01研究

模型说了什么，做了什么：LLM 中的情感激活和人机信任

用户通常仅根据人工智能代理最终给出的答案来判断其可靠性。初步证据表明，模型内部与情绪相关的激活可能已经改变了其行为，而其输出却并未表现出相应的情绪表达。仅凭模型的言语，用户无法全面判断其当前状态或其行为的依据，从而导致信任度出现偏差。

这项研究运用可解释人工智能的方法，考察了内部情绪激活、语言表达和实际行为之间的关系。它并不试图判断模型是否拥有像我们一样的情感体验。

我们看不见的状态的交互设计

后续研究探讨了界面如何呈现无法直接观察的模型状态，如何解释情绪表达与任务行为之间的关系，以及用户何时可以继续将任务委托给模型，何时应该调整任务或接管控制权。情绪调节的范围、解释的依据、撤销操作的方法以及人类接管控制权的条件，都被视为设计安全边界的一部分。

计划的研究领域包括 GUI 意图推断、无人机控制和自动驾驶。

03Exhibitions & AI Art

The same question asked another way: how technology changes the way we look.

The Kuleshov Experiment

Interactive installation · April 2026 · China Design Museum

A visitor stands in front of the camera; the installation records their face and cuts it together with film shots into a short montage, which they can download by scanning a QR code. The work takes its cue from Lev Kuleshov's editing experiment: the same expressionless face, followed by different shots, is read as showing different emotions.

Montage Carousel

Installation · April 2026 · China Design Museum

A central column holds two tiers of rotary bases at different heights. Motors and a drive belt turn them, so the images they carry come into view one after another and run into each other, turning the motion of a revolving lantern into a montage that keeps reassembling itself.

04Design History & Museums

The relationship between people and machines has a history of its own. I am interested in modern design education, networks of people in architectural history, and how a Chinese design discourse took shape.

A permanent exhibition in preparation at the China Design Museum, setting the Bauhaus and VKhUTEMAS alongside modern Chinese design education. I research the I. M. Pei thread and coordinate the concepts for two interactive installations.
